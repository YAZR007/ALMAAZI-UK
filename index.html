Haute Horlogerie & Joaillerie Standards

The Almaazi Standard of Savoir-Faire

Behind every Almaazi creation lies over 180 hours of meticulous hand-faceting, invisible micro-pavé setting under high-magnification optics, and unwavering dedication to perfection.

I

Ethical Provenance & Kimberley Process

100% of our rough diamonds and gemstones are traced to conflict-free, responsible mines in Botswana, Canada, and Colombia.

II

Hearts & Arrows Ideal Optical Symmetry

Cut to mathematically exact proportions to unleash maximum internal light dispersion, fire, and scintillating scintillation.

III

Invisible French Micro-Pavé Setting

Metals are hollowed to microscopic tolerances, ensuring stones are held by delicate beads allowing light to enter from 360 degrees.
